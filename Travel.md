# 1. Interplanetary Transit Times

Route timing at the network-standard drive spec ([Technology §1.1](Technology.md#1.1)). See [Technology §1](Technology.md#1) for the propulsion and vehicle engineering behind these figures.

| Route | Distance | Transit time (at std. drive spec) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yemoja ↔ Olokun (Obatala) | ~9.05–9.95 AU ([Cosmology §5](Cosmology.md#5)) | ~90 days (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 732 km/s ΔV — see [Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4)) |
| Lugh ↔ Boann/Mannannán (Danu) | ~5.85 AU | ~70.6 days (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 573.9 km/s ΔV — see [Technology §1.2](Technology.md#1.2)) |
| Safford (Mannannán moon) → Lugh (Danu) | ~5.85 AU | ~70.6 days (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 573.9 km/s ΔV — see [Technology §1.2](Technology.md#1.2)) |
| Safford (Olokun moon) → Yemoja (Obatala) | ~9.05–9.95 AU (Safford co-orbits Olokun, [Cosmology §5.3](Cosmology.md#5.3)) | ~90 days (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 732 km/s ΔV — see [Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4)) |
| Safford (rubble pile, ~0.05 AU) → Ereshkigal (Enlil) | ~0.035 AU | ~48 hours (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 79.7 km/s ΔV, spin-gravity — see [Technology §1.3](Technology.md#1.3)) |

*Both long-haul routes now share the same continuous-thrust brachistochrone ITV design ([Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4)/[§1.4.1](Technology.md#1.4.1)) rather than separate architectures — Lugh's shorter distance gives it a faster, cheaper crossing on the identical hull (70.6 days / 573.9 km/s vs. Yemoja's 90 days / 732 km/s). For reference, a fuel-optimal (Hohmann) transfer between Yemoja and Olokun costs only ~22.1 km/s total ΔV but takes ~6.0 years transit — worked in full at [Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4).*
