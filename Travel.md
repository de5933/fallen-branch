# 1. Interplanetary Transit Times

Route timing at the network-standard drive spec ([Technology §1.1](Technology.md#1.1)). See [Technology §1](Technology.md#1) for the propulsion and vehicle engineering behind these figures.

| Route | Distance | Transit time (at std. drive spec) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yemoja ↔ Olokun (Obatala) | ~9.05–9.95 AU ([Cosmology §5](Cosmology.md#5)) | ~90 days (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 732 km/s ΔV — see [Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4)) |
| Lugh ↔ Boann/Mannannán (Danu) | ~5.85 AU | under 2 months |
| Safford (Mannannán moon) → Lugh (Danu) | ~5.85 AU | ~1.7–1.9 months |
| Safford (Olokun moon) → Yemoja (Obatala) | ~9.05–9.95 AU (Safford co-orbits Olokun, [Cosmology §5.3](Cosmology.md#5.3)) | ~90 days (continuous-thrust brachistochrone, 732 km/s ΔV — see [Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4)) |
| Safford (rubble pile, ~0.05 AU) → Ereshkigal (Enlil) | ~0.035 AU | under 2 days |

*For reference, a fuel-optimal (Hohmann) transfer between Yemoja and Olokun costs only ~22.1 km/s total ΔV but takes ~6.0 years transit — worked in full at [Technology §1.4](Technology.md#1.4). The continuous-thrust brachistochrone actually used for this route covers the same distance in ~90 days at a much higher 732 km/s ΔV cost — a different trade than the Danu route's accelerate-coast-decelerate profile ([Technology §1.1](Technology.md#1.1)), made necessary by Yemoja's much greater distance from Safford.*
