# Nova Reach Network — Master Timeline

*All events currently established in the Worldbuilding Bible (`setting.md`), in chronological order. Years are relative to Nova Reach's founding (Year 0). Ranges reflect the bible's own uncertainty, not missing research.*

| Year / Range | Event | Details | Bible Ref. |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~Year -15 | Intra-Sol gate program begins | Farfield proves gate technology on safe, nearby Sol-system targets over roughly a decade and a half, before risking a full expansion aimed at an unknown, unreachable-by-any-other-means destination. | §8 |
| Pre-Year 0 (undated) | Saturn ring-seed program finds an exceptional resonance band | Farfield's seed-harvesting program in Saturn's rings identifies an exceptional resonance band. Systematic Stage 1 peeks begin across many harvested seeds; most are discarded. A handful reach Stage 2 characterization. | §3, §8 |
| **Year 0** | **Nova Reach founded** | The 39th gate inflated by Farfield (Sat-39) reaches Stage 2, revealing an exceptionally rich ring-band and giant-planet system. Farfield commits to full Stage 3 expansion. Sat-39 opens; first-wave colonists (administrators plus idealists) step through. | §6, §8 |
| ~Year 0–25 | Chronic Sol→colony trade imbalance (unconfirmed working theory) | For roughly the first quarter of Nova Reach's history, nearly all gate traffic runs Sol-to-colony, with little of comparable value flowing back until local Trelium production and seed-harvesting exist to export — a candidate contributing cause of Sat-39's eventual mass drift and collapse, not confirmed in-universe. | §9.2 |
| ~Year 5–10 | Ramp-up | Second-wave pragmatist colonists arrive once word reaches Earth about real conditions; the extraction pipeline scales up. | §8, §10.1 |
| ~Year 25 | Hub emergence — Sat-48 and the three branch systems founded | Nova Reach's own harvested seeds are peeked, characterized, and (for three of them) fully expanded locally. Sat-48 goes up. Danu, Obatala, and Enlil are discovered and founded, each Safford wing sited on its own local body. In-system transit/comm-delay geography begins shaping colony governance from this point on. | §7, §8 |
| ~Year 25–92 | Mature hub / steady state | Danu, Obatala, and Enlil each mature into distinct colonies with their own character, at their own pace. Comm-delay geography splits each system into a fast-coupled cluster (Safford–Boann in Danu; Safford–Ereshkigal in Enlil) and a slow-coupled one (Lugh in Danu; Olokun in Obatala), pushing the slow-coupled settlements toward early independent local decision-making well before the severance. | §8, §15.1 |
| Year 36–92 | Pre-severance steady state | Stable, extractive, universally resented, and completely functional. Farfield's amoral efficiency is normalized; resentment calcifies into resignation. Everyone knows the system is evil; nobody expects it to change. | §10.6 |
| **Year 92** | **The Severance Event — Sat-39 collapses** | Sat-39 fails on the Sol side (cause unknown/unknowable in-universe). Both mouths convert to black holes, each carrying the mass that mouth held at the moment of failure — a sudden, violent burst of hard radiation at both ends. By this point founders are dead or very elderly; their grandchildren and great-grandchildren run the colonies. | §8, §9, §9.1 |
| Year 92 (moment of collapse) | News propagates outward at light speed | Nova Reach knows immediately; branch systems learn nearly as fast via gate-to-gate fiber threading. In-system populations learn only after a further light-speed delay — Danu's Safford almost instantly, Lugh 48–51 minutes later, Olokun 2–3 hours later. | §15.2 |
| Year 92, Days 1–60 | Phase 1 — Conservative leadership | The highest-ranking Nova Reach administrator at collapse becomes de facto leader and takes a cautious approach: stabilizing internal systems, sending diplomatic envoys to Danu, Enlil, and Obatala in hopes of a cooperative federation. Real-time coordination is undercut from day one by comm lag (1.6–1.7 hr round trip to Lugh alone). This window of restraint lets Danu consolidate idealist control and seize Boann's water facilities. | §12.1 |
| Year 92+ (after Day 60) | Phase 2 — Assassination and military escalation | The conservative leader is assassinated by a Nova Reach insider sympathetic to Danu's independence movement. A hardline militaristic faction seizes the opening, blames Danu for "sabotage," and mobilizes hastily-armed civilian security forces into military incursions against Danu. Danu's defenders (engineers, maintenance crews, hazard workers) inflict multiple costly defeats using home-turf knowledge and improvised weapons; Nova Reach's stale, lag-delayed orders read as hostile intent on both sides. | §12.2 |
| Year 92+ (following Phase 2) | Phase 3 — Negotiated peace | After repeated failed incursions, the militaristic leader steps down or is removed by pragmatist factions. A moderate leader accepts Danu's independence as the cost of stability. Danu is formally recognized as independent; borders and minimal trade relations are established. Nova Reach turns to consolidating Enlil and Obatala and developing Obatala's agriculture as an alternative food source to Danu. | §12.3 |
| Year 92+ (concurrent with Phases 1–3) | Local leadership and negotiation period | Across each branch system, local leaders emerge by necessity (often whoever already held practical authority), then negotiate — sometimes via a single spokesperson — directly with Nova Reach over Safford access, in parallel with the military arc rather than strictly after it. Concrete choices made during this window (resource sharing vs. hoarding, treatment of stranded Nova Reach personnel, use of chaos to settle old scores, substance of Nova Reach's outreach) determine each system's eventual relationship with Nova Reach. | §11.0 |
| Post-Year 92 (settled equilibrium) | Two-entity political division solidifies | The network fractures into the **Danu Democratic Republic** (independent, democratic, resource-constrained — reliant on closed-loop phosphorus recycling and H₂/O₂ rockets) and the **Farfield Technocratic Zone** (Nova Reach, Enlil, and Obatala under centralized, technocratic corporate-style rule). Enlil remains a dependent corporate autocracy under tight, genuinely real-time Nova Reach control; Obatala develops a presidential system with autonomous sectors. The two entities maintain an uneasy peace: Danu is independent but resource-constrained, Farfield cannot afford the cost of conquering Danu. | §11.1–§11.5 |
| Post-Year 92 (ongoing) | A martyr emerges | A charismatic leader leads early peaceful rebuilding efforts before being assassinated, becoming a lasting martyr/symbol of hope for stability. (Likely the same figure as the assassinated Phase 1 conservative leader, §12.2 — final characterization not yet locked.) | §10.6 |
| Undated (background physics, pre-story, cosmic timescale) | Ongoing raw-seed loss channel | Stellar cores, supernova remnants, and magnetar magnetospheres occasionally destroy raw primordial seeds outright via violent charge-exchange, invisibly thinning the surviving seed population over cosmic time. No bearing on the story's timeline. | §2 |
| Sequel/epilogue-horizon (well beyond this story's timeline) | Manufactured wormholes and the Chronology Protection test | Multiple fundamental breakthroughs beyond Sat-48-era engineering eventually make *manufacturing* a wormhole mouth (rather than harvesting a primordial one) possible. The first attempt to give a manufactured pair's mouths diverging velocity/gravitational histories (the first real shot at a closed timelike curve) is answered by vacuum-fluctuation runaway destroying the throat before causality violation becomes possible — confirming Hawking's Chronology Protection Conjecture at the cost of a destroyed lab and research team. Forward-looking lore only; not intended to be dramatized on-page in the current novel. | §2.2 |

## Open dating questions

- Exact triggering mechanism/date detail for Sat-39's collapse beyond "Year 92" is not yet decided as canon (a chronic mass-imbalance theory exists but is explicitly unconfirmed in-universe).
- Precise day-counts for Phase 2's assassination and Phase 3's resolution (beyond "after Day 60") are not yet fixed.
- The martyred charismatic leader's relationship to the Phase 1 conservative leader is implied but not explicitly confirmed as the same individual.
